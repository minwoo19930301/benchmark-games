# Asset credits

## Pokémon Red / Blue battle sprites

The ten front/back battle sprite PNGs in `public/assets/pokemon/` are bundled, unchanged, from the [PokéAPI sprites repository](https://github.com/PokeAPI/sprites), generation I `red-blue` folders. They show Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Pidgey and Rattata. Exact source URLs are recorded in [sources.json](../public/assets/pokemon/sources.json).

The upstream notice states: “All image contents within are Copyright The Pokémon Company.” The repository license is retained in [LICENSE.txt](../public/assets/pokemon/LICENSE.txt). We do not claim ownership of these images. The game's world tiles, menus, simulation and fallback drawings are implemented in this repository. It is an unofficial browser reconstruction, not a Nintendo release.

Other cartridge models, animation rigs, scenery, pixel drawings and sound effects are authored in code unless an additional entry is recorded here. Reference titles and characters belong to their respective owners.

## Metal Slug

Marco animation atlas and Mission 1 background: [zzarcon/html5-slug](https://github.com/zzarcon/html5-slug/tree/master/game/assets). Additional Marco action poses and explosion animation: [nathancy/Metal-Slug](https://github.com/nathancy/Metal-Slug/tree/master/Metal_Slug). Rebel rifle soldier, POW and jungle background: [alejru08/Metal-Slug](https://github.com/alejru08/Metal-Slug/tree/master/images). Tetsuyuki boss: [Diddylcp/MetalSlugPhaser](https://github.com/Diddylcp/MetalSlugPhaser/tree/main/images/mission1/finalBoss). Original artwork belongs to SNK; these public fan-project mirrors establish provenance, not ownership or a license from SNK. We do not claim to have drawn those images. Files are stored unchanged, with source links and hashes in `public/assets/metalslug/sources.json`; the Marco atlas metadata is retained. SV-001 idle and moving sprites: [hashir249/MetalSlug](https://github.com/hashir249/MetalSlug/tree/main/assets/textures). Game simulation, level collision geometry, fallback vehicle drawings and HUD are written for this project. No game ROM, executable or soundtrack is included.

## Mega Man X4 character art

X animation PNGs: [wabtytsai/Fakeman-X4, Sprites](https://github.com/wabtytsai/Fakeman-X4/tree/master/Sprites). Zero atlas: [surcae/MegaMan-X4](https://github.com/surcae/MegaMan-X4/blob/master/RockMan_imitation/Resource/Pure/Pure/zerox4sheet.gif). Original character artwork belongs to Capcom; the mirrors provide provenance rather than an ownership or license transfer. The image files remain unchanged, with individual source links and SHA-256 in `public/assets/reploid/SOURCES.json`. Their frames are selected by this project's simulation. Boss drawings, scenery, physics and fallback character drawings are procedural; X4 art is shared across the three compact X4/X5/X6 stages and is not a claim to reproduce each game's exact armor or animation catalog.
